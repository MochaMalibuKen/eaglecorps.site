About Eagle Corps Services

Legacy-driven consulting and mission support for government-aligned operations.

Our story

Eagle Corps Services was founded by a veteran leader with extensive experience in safety, security, fire response, emergency operations, and mission-critical program delivery. The company evolved from operational leadership into a broader mission of helping organizations improve safety, preparedness, quality, compliance, and operational performance.

Today, under the leadership of Mary Allen, Eagle Corps Services continues the founding vision while expanding into modern quality systems, preparedness solutions, government contract support, and NQA-1-related services.

Core values

Integrity, service, and performance shape every engagement.

Eagle Corps Services integrates customer commitments naturally into our work: we listen, we respect those we support, we steward safety, and we deliver results the first time. Our work is grounded in a thoughtful, practical approach to compliance, operational readiness, and mission assurance.
